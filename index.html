What is PTSD?

PTSD stands for Post-Traumatic Stress Disorder. It is a mental health condition that can develop after someone has experienced or witnessed a traumatic event.

The symptoms can include:

Traumatic flashbacks

Re-experiencing the traumatic event in vivid detail, feeling as if the event is happening all over again.

Anxiety

Feeling constantly on edge, worried, or apprehensive, with an inability to relax.

Depression

Feeling sad, hopeless, empty, or disconnected from others.

Irritability

Feeling angry, restless, or on edge, with an increased sensitivity to stimuli.

Hypervigilance

Being constantly aware of one's surroundings, with a sense of being "on the lookout".

Avoidance

Avoiding people, places, or activities that remind one of the traumatic event.

Where do I go from here?

The hardest part is often just deciding that you deserve to feel better. You don't have to figure everything out today, and you don't have to do it all at once. Whether it's talking to a friend or finding a professional, the goal is simply to find what makes you feel safe.
